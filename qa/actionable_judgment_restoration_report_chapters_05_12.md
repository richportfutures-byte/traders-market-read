## Audit Standard

**Actionable in judgment, non-executional in instruction.**

A glossary entry may explain what a trader should watch, downgrade, confirm, invalidate, classify, monitor, or label, and how a condition affects read quality, setup quality, location quality, thesis state, confirmation requirements, invalidation clarity, expression quality, or operator posture. It must not prescribe exact entries, exits, stops, targets, sizing, adds, reductions, broker actions, account behavior, fills, or P&L behavior. Market-participant behavior (shorts cover, longs liquidate, sellers press, defenders lose control, forced buying appears) is valid market-read language and is not flagged.
